1. EXECUTIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

GeoSentinel is an AI-assisted geospatial intelligence platform designed to detect
mining expansion, land-use changes, and vegetation disturbance, combining them
with satellite thermal intelligence and microclimate environmental data to
identify and prioritize high-risk zones.

The Core Problem:
Traditional environmental monitoring either focuses strictly on optical change
detection (deforestation, bare ground) without understanding thermal impact, or
displays raw satellite heatmaps without contextualizing where human disturbance
is actually occurring.

The Core Question Answered by GeoSentinel:
"Where is the environmental risk highest, and where should ground investigation
and inspection teams be prioritized first?"

3. MULTI-SENSOR DATA ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

IMPORTANT REMOTE SENSING DISTINCTION:
Sentinel-2 does NOT have thermal infrared sensors. It operates across visible,
near-infrared, and shortwave infrared (up to 2.2 microns). Land Surface
Temperature (LST) requires thermal infrared (10.4 - 12.5 microns), which is
captured by Landsat 8/9 TIRS or MODIS. GeoSentinel explicitly separates these
sources.

DATA SOURCES SPECIFICATION:

1. SENTINEL-2 (Optical & Vegetation Disturbance)
   - Source: European Space Agency (ESA) Copernicus Program
   - Instrument: MultiSpectral Instrument (MSI), Level-2A (Surface Reflectance)
   - Bands Required:
     * Band 4 (Red): 665 nm central wavelength, 10m spatial resolution
     * Band 8 (NIR - Near Infrared): 842 nm, 10m spatial resolution
   - Temporal Requirements:
     * T1 (Baseline date, e.g., 12-24 months prior, cloud cover < 5%)
     * T2 (Current/recent monitoring date, cloud cover < 5%)
   - Computed Products: NDVI(T1), NDVI(T2), dNDVI (Delta NDVI)

2. LANDSAT 8 / 9 (Thermal Land Surface Temperature)
   - Source: USGS / NASA Landsat Program
   - Instrument: Thermal Infrared Sensor (TIRS-1 / TIRS-2)
   - Product: Level-2 Surface Temperature (ST) Derived Product
   - Bands / Channels: Band 10 (Thermal Infrared: 10.60 - 11.19 microns)
   - Native Resolution: 100m (resampled to 30m in standard Level-2 products)
   - Temporal Requirements: Acquired close to T2 date (same season/month)
   - Computed Products:
     * Current LST (degrees Celsius)
     * Local Vegetated Baseline LST (degrees Celsius)
     * Thermal Anomaly dT = LST_pixel - LST_baseline

3. FORTYGUARD / HYPERLOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL LAYER (Pluggable Microclimate Layer)
   - Source: FortyGuard Microclimate API or Sample Ground Temperature Grid
   - Resolution: 1m to 10m hyperlocal spatial resolution
   - Status in MVP: Clearly labeled as "Sample Environmental Grid"
   - Role: Provides micro-temperature gradients within open pit areas,
     tailings ponds, and machinery clusters.
   - Modularity: Connected via a standardized TypeScript/Python adapter
     interface so real API keys or data payloads can be swapped without
     altering the core risk engine.

4. SPATIAL RESOLUTION ALIGNMENT STRATEGY (10m vs. 30m / 100m)
================================================================================

Challenge:
Sentinel-2 optical pixels are 10m x 10m.
Landsat thermal pixels are 30m x 30m (resampled from 100m).

MVP Solution — The Zonal Statistics Aggregation Method:
Rather than attempting complex pixel-level super-resolution or bilinear
resampling (which can introduce spatial artifacts), GeoSentinel uses vector-based
zonal aggregation:

1. Step 1 (Vector Delineation at 10m):
   High-change Sentinel-2 pixels (where dNDVI >= 0.25) are clustered and
   converted into vector polygon boundaries using GeoPandas / Shapely.
   This preserves crisp, accurate boundaries of actual excavated ground.

2. Step 2 (Zonal Thermal Sampling at 30m):
   For each delineated 10m polygon zone, compute the mean, maximum, and standard
   deviation of the underlying Landsat 8/9 Surface Temperature raster.

3. Step 3 (Hyperlocal Overlay if available):
   If sample FortyGuard microclimate points fall within the polygon, compute the
   micro-anomaly score to enrich the zone profile.

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS & EXPLAINABLE RISK SCORING ENGINE
================================================================================

A. VEGETATION INDEX & LAND DISTURBANCE (Sentinel-2, 10m)

   NDVI(T1) = (B8_T1 - B4_T1) / (B8_T1 + B4_T1)
   NDVI(T2) = (B8_T2 - B4_T2) / (B8_T2 + B4_T2)

   dNDVI = NDVI(T1) - NDVI(T2)

   Interpretation:
   * dNDVI >= +0.25  : Significant vegetation clearing / stripped overburden
   * -0.10 <= dNDVI <= +0.10 : Stable land cover
   * dNDVI < -0.10   : Regrowth / vegetation recovery

B. THERMAL ANOMALY CALCULATION (Landsat 8/9 LST, 30m)

   LST_baseline = Median LST of undisturbed surrounding canopy at T2
   dT (Thermal Anomaly) = LST_zone_mean - LST_baseline

   Interpretation:
   * dT < +1.5 C     : Normal thermal response
   * +1.5 C <= dT < +4.0 C : Moderate thermal increase (exposed soil/rock)
   * dT >= +4.0 C    : High thermal anomaly (bare excavation, dark tailings, machinery)

C. EXPLAINABLE ENVIRONMENTAL RISK SCORE FORMULA (0 - 100)

   The Risk Score combines vegetation disturbance, thermal anomaly, and
   contiguous impacted surface area into a single bounded score:

   Risk Score = MIN(100,  50 * S_veg + 35 * S_thermal + 15 * S_area)

   Where:

   1. S_veg (Vegetation Disturbance Factor, range 0.0 to 1.0):
      S_veg = CLAMP((dNDVI - 0.15) / (0.55 - 0.15), 0.0, 1.0)

   2. S_thermal (Thermal Anomaly Factor, range 0.0 to 1.0):
      S_thermal = CLAMP((dT - 1.5) / (7.5 - 1.5), 0.0, 1.0)

   3. S_area (Spatial Footprint Factor, range 0.0 to 1.0):
      S_area = CLAMP(Area_in_Hectares / 40.0, 0.0, 1.0)

   (CLAMP(x, min, max) restricts x between min and max)

D. RISK CLASSIFICATION TIERS

   +----------------+------------+---------------------------------------------+
   | Classification | Score Band | Operational Recommendation                  |
   +----------------+------------+---------------------------------------------+
   | CRITICAL       | 80 - 100   | Immediate Priority: Urgent field inspection |
   | HIGH           | 60 - 79    | Priority 2: Scheduled investigation        |
   | MEDIUM         | 35 - 59    | Priority 3: Monitor next satellite cycle    |
   | LOW            | 0 - 34     | Baseline: Normal operational variation      |
   +----------------+------------+---------------------------------------------+

7. MODULAR ENVIRONMENTAL / FORTYGUARD INTERFACE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

To ensure zero vendor lock-in and prevent reliance on private APIs during the
hackathon demo, the data contract separates data sources explicitly:

TypeScript Data Interfaces:

```typescript
export type RiskTier = 'LOW' | 'MEDIUM' | 'HIGH' | 'CRITICAL';

export interface ThermalSourceMeta {
  sourceType: 'LANDSAT_8_TIRS' | 'FORTYGUARD_SAMPLE_GRID' | 'HYBRID';
  resolutionMeters: number;
  isSampleData: boolean; // Explicit label for hackathon transparency
  unit: 'celsius';
}

export interface ZoneMetrics {
  zoneId: string;
  name: string;
  riskScore: number; // 0 to 100
  riskTier: RiskTier;
  
  // Sentinel-2 Optical Metrics (10m)
  sentinel: {
    baselineDate: string;
    currentDate: string;
    ndviBaseline: number;
    ndviCurrent: number;
    deltaNdvi: number;
    areaHectares: number;
  };
  
  // Landsat & Environmental Thermal Metrics
  thermal: {
    acquisitionDate: string;
    currentLstC: number;
    baselineLstC: number;
    thermalAnomalyDeltaC: number;
    sourceMeta: ThermalSourceMeta;
  };
  
  // Plain-English Rationale
  explanation: {
    primaryDriver: string;
    detailedSummary: string;
    recommendedAction: string;
  };
}
```

8. FRONTEND INTERACTIVE MAP & UX DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

A. Viewport Layout:
   - Fixed, non-scrolling single-screen command center.
   - Header Bar: Study Area Name, Bounding Box, Satellite Acquisition Dates (T1 vs T2),
     and KPI Summary Badges (Total Monitored Area, Active Zones, Critical Alerts).
   - Left / Right Panel: Ranked Prioritization Queue (sorted by Risk Score desc).
   - Center / Main Stage: Leaflet Map with smooth interactive pan/zoom.
   - Slide-out Inspector Drawer: Opens when a polygon or leaderboard item is clicked.

B. Map Layer Switcher:
   1. Base Maps: Satellite Imagery vs. Clean Carto Dark/Light.
   2. Sentinel-2 T1 Baseline Optical Layer.
   3. Sentinel-2 T2 Current Optical Layer.
   4. Landsat 8/9 Thermal LST Heatmap Layer.
   5. Risk Zone Polygons Layer (Color-coded: Red=Critical, Orange=High, Yellow=Medium, Green=Low).

C. Zone Inspector Drawer Details:
   - Overall Risk Score Badge with color-coded tier.
   - Side-by-side Metric Cards:
     * Vegetation Disturbance: dNDVI value, % drop, baseline vs current.
     * Thermal Anomaly: Zone LST vs Baseline LST, delta T (degrees C).
     * Disturbance Footprint: Area in Hectares and Square Kilometers.
   - Visual Comparison Bar: Relative contribution of Vegetation Loss vs Thermal vs Area.
   - Explainable Inspector Notes: Clear rationale of why this zone scored high.

MILESTONE 3: Explainable Risk Engine & GeoJSON Generation

14. SCOPE BOUNDARIES: FEATURES EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED FROM MVP
================================================================================

- NO User authentication, passwords, or signup forms.
- NO Relational database setup (PostgreSQL / PostGIS).
- NO Black-box deep learning or unexplainable neural networks.
- NO Global coordinate search or multi-region selector (focus on 1 high-impact study area).
- NO Live satellite streaming connections that risk failing during demo Wi-Fi.
- NO Complex administrative billing or RBAC role management.
